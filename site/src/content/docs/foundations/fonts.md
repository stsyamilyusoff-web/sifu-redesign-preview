**Circular Std** is the app typeface. Allowed weights: **Book, Medium, Bold** only - Black and numeric weights 600/800 are banned, and `fontWeight` overrides on Circular families are not allowed.

Canon: the font-audit-wave1 document (§10) in the design-system repo; sentence-case eyebrows per the 07-Jul font law sweep.
