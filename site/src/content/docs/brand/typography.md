---
title: Typography (brand)
description: The Circular Std law.
---

**Circular Std** is the typeface. Allowed weights: **Book, Medium, Bold** only. Black and numeric weights 600/800 are banned, and `fontWeight` overrides on Circular families are not allowed. Eyebrows are sentence-case. Canon: font-audit-wave1 §10 in the design-system repo.
