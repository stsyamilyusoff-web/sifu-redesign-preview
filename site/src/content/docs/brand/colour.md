---
title: Colour (brand)
description: The live marketing palette and its relationship to app colour.
---

The live brand palette (from sifututor.my and Instagram): indigo `#3b4ee2`, petrol ink `#00262a`, gold `#f5bc00`, pale-indigo tint `#eaeefc`. The tone is warm and lightly playful, never gig-hustle.

In the **product**, colour is adopted at the token level as the locked app ramp (see [Foundations · Colour tokens](/sifu-redesign-preview/foundations/colour-tokens/)). Gold and decorative brand elements stay in marketing.
