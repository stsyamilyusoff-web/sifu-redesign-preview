Rules for the integrator (ratified 02-Jul-2026)

1 · Subject is the anchor. Render only when the subject matches (subjects_taught contains subject_id, EDC flag). No subject match, no line, even if schedule or area coincide.
2 · Order and trimming. Display order subject → level → schedule → area. Cap at 3 reasons; when trimming, keep by value: subject, then level, then area, then schedule.
3 · Grammar. Two reasons: "A & B". Three: "A, B & C". Sentence case, no terminal punctuation, one line tall, never truncate (trim reasons instead).
4 · Relationship override. When the live Recommended logic hits SameParent or SameStudent, the line is "You already teach this family" and attribute reasons are dropped; one MatchLine per card, ever.
5 · Never reasons. Gender (tutor_preference) and mode are filters per DR-007, hidden not celebrated. Integrator flag: the live feed does not filter gender yet.
6 · Gated reasons ship dark. "Area" waits on the tutor-side preferred-cities field (Hafiz Q11); "level" rides SameLevel teaching history until a levels-taught field exists. The component accepts both as data today.
7 · Visual. Text + Phosphor check (regular weight, 13px box) in #166534, weight 700, 12.5px, no fill, no border, 7px above-gap in the card stack. Green stays rationed: MatchLine, the verified seal, and "Be the first to apply" are the only greens a card may carry.
