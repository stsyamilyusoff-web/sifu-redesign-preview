---
title: Colour
description: The locked app colour ramp and how it relates to the marketing brand.
---

**App accent ramp (locked):** `#2B2FBE` (primary indigo) with `#243cb3` as the deep step. One accent per screen; the app is the calm workshop bench.

**Marketing brand (the shopfront):** the website and social use a brighter palette (indigo `#3b4ee2`, petrol ink `#00262a`, gold `#f5bc00`, pale-indigo tint `#eaeefc`). Brand is adopted in the app at the **token level only** - one accent, ink, type, warm copy - never the marketing layout noise (colour-flood heroes, gold badges, decorative shapes).
