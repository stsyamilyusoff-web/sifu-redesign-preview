The craft
ShapeGrey blocks that mirror the real row/card (avatar circle + title bar + meta bar + trailing).
MotionPulse opacity 55→100% ~1.5s ease-in-out. NO shimmer sweep (that’s a gradient, off our flat brand).
ColourNeutral #E7EBF0, never indigo. A placeholder isn’t branded content.
Count3-5 rows/cards to fill the viewport; dissolves into real content ~200ms.
InlineA small spinner/dots only for short inline waits, skeleton for lists + content screens.
Kept distinct: PULSE + no CTA = loading; STATIC + an icon + a CTA = empty (“nothing here”). Never confuse the two.
Tokens: block #E7EBF0 · canvas #EDF0F9 · card #FFF · radius 16 (card) / 5 (bars) / 7 (trailing) · pulse 1.5s.
