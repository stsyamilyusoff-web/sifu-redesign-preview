---
title: Voice and copy
description: The standing copy laws.
---

- **Neutral tone**: no negative or positive leading; ask plainly and let the data flag issues.
- **No em-dashes**, anywhere, ever.
- Descriptions are **one concise paragraph**; never stack double explanations.
- Support submissions are **"tickets"** (never "requests", which are jobs).
- **No explainer sub-text under CTAs**; the button label stands alone.
- One date format per surface; whole-RM money except exact-reconciliation screens.
