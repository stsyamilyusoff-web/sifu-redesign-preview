Trust primitive · verified badge + tier standing

Lane C candidate set. Locked rule: rich on the tutor's own profile, lean everywhere else. No star rating exists in the data, so the standing is the tier system, never stars. Trust here is recognition and motivation for the tutor, not a sales badge.
Grounded in origin/main of sifututor_tutor: verified = tutorDetail.status === 'verified' (today: plain text + icon next to the name in ProfileMenu) · standing = profile_strength_score (0 to 100) + profile_strength_tier (beginner · intermediate · advanced · all_star, labels Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced / All star, flag-gated by enhanced_data_collection_v1) · app tier accents kept: Beginner #F97316 · Intermediate #EAB308 · Advanced #22C55E · All star #D4AF37 · "Verified hours" already exists on the dashboard.

Sub-decision A · seal colour

Green seal
Follows our own rule, green = verified / achieved only. Reads as a status, not a brand mark.
Indigo seal
What Airbnb does (badge in brand pink). Reads as "the platform vouches", but spends the indigo budget on a non-action.

Sub-decision B · tier glyphs + colours (all four tiers)

B1 · keep the app's gamification accents
Beginner Intermediate Advanced All star
Continuity with the live app (orange, yellow, green, gold). But Advanced green collides with the verified green.
B2 · restrained: neutral tiers, gold only at the top
Beginner Intermediate Advanced All star
Fits the locked colour budget (restrained accent). Gold stays special for the top tier, no green collision. Less playful than today.
