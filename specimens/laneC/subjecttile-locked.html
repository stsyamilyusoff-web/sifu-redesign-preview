Rules for the integrator (ratified 02-Jul-2026)

1 · The DR-005 gate. Icon only when clear + distinguishable from siblings + culturally neutral; when in doubt, monogram. Religious, moral, and history subjects are always monograms; never a religious or cultural symbol.
2 · Keying. Map by subject_id (from tutor/curricula → subjects[]), never the name string. Unmapped id → the slate open-book fallback.
3 · Geometry. 64 tile in picker grids (label below, outside the tile, 2 lines max) · 40 tile inline on rows · radius 14 · mark ≈ 47% of tile.
4 · Art. One commissioned flat set, single stroke weight (~3.5 at 64), family-ink on family-tint. The stand-ins in the candidates sheet define composition, so build now and swap art without layout changes. Monograms: Circular Std Bold, ~31% of tile, max 2 letters.
5 · Colour discipline. Family colours exist ONLY inside the tile (ground + mark). Never on text, pills, buttons, or icons outside the tile; fit green, flag amber, and action indigo stay the only UI status colours. Exact family hexes finalize in Lane A's token pass; the MAPPING is what is locked.
6 · One map everywhere. Picker, class cards, records, reports all render the same tile; no surface invents its own subject art. The DR-004 grey anchor watermark is a separate MoneyAnchor-owned rule.
