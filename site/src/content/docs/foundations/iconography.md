Feature and semantic icons are **Phosphor outline**, per the banked [icons element](https://stsyamilyusoff-web.github.io/sifu-redesign-preview/elements/icons.html); utility marks stay simple.

A **Solar bold-duotone** alternative was explored (a 17-treatment open-source survey) and **declined** by the founder after a current-vs-proposed side-by-side on the real approved set. The exploration stays on record in the element library; the icon system is unchanged.
