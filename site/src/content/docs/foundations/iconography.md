Icons are **Solar bold-duotone** (founder-locked at element bank #16): filled two-tone glyphs that give depth without a colour flood. The lock followed a 17-treatment open-source survey after the flat outline set read as boring and predictable; the no-results glyph uses Solar's minimalistic-magnifier variant.

Migration of the earlier banked elements and built screens to Solar bold-duotone is in progress; the superseded flat-outline exploration stays on record in the [element library](https://stsyamilyusoff-web.github.io/sifu-redesign-preview/components/elements/).
