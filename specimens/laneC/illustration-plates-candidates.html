Fork P1 · style register (shown on the requests motif)

P1a · monoline on tint plate
Indigo line drawing, same 2px-family weight as Phosphor. Quietest, cheapest to commission, risks reading as "just a big icon".
P1b · duotone + sparkle
Same line, one tint fill inside the motif, one quiet sparkle. Reads as crafted illustration, still a single-colour system.
P1c · mini scene
Duotone plus background shapes (dots, ground line). Warmest, most Airbnb; biggest art budget and busiest at 96px.

Rules to ratify (say if any feels wrong)

1 · Geometry. Plate = 96px, radius 28 squircle, tint ground; motif ≈ 64px inside. One size everywhere (the Lane B EmptyState slot).
2 · Scope. Plates appear ONLY in empty states. ErrorState keeps the plain icon (Lane B lock). Never on banners, cards, or success screens (success has its own varied designs).
3 · One commissioned set. Six plates drawn together, one stroke weight and corner language, shared with the SubjectTile art set so the app has ONE illustration voice. The drawings above define motif + composition only.
4 · The tint home. StatusBanner retired tinted cards; the plate ground is where tint lives. Fit green and flag amber never appear in plates.
5 · Copy stays locked elsewhere. Titles, bodies, CTAs, and the filter-aware requests copy belong to the state-system + Lane B locks; contexts with sub-states (requests: no-supply vs filtered-empty) reuse ONE plate per context.
6 · Fallback. A generic plate (the inbox motif, neutral) for any future context until its plate is commissioned; never an empty gap.
