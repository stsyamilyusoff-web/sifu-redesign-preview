---
title: Brand
description: The Sifututor identity, and the law for how brand enters the product.
---

Sifututor keeps two layers, the way Apple and Uber do: the **brand** (the shopfront: website, social, marketing) is warm and loud; the **product** (the apps: the workshop bench) is calm HIG discipline. Brand enters the app **at the token level only**: one accent, ink, type, warm copy. Marketing layout noise (colour-flood heroes, gold badges, decorative shapes) never crosses into the product.
