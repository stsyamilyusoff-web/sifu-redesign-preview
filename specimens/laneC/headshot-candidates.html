Rules to ratify (say if any feels wrong)

1 · Size ramp. 24 inline mention · 40 header (live) · 56 card · 76 profile hero. No other sizes; the seal overlap exists only at 76 (Trust lock).
2 · Initials. Max 2 letters from display_name (first + last word), Circular Std Medium, ~38% of the circle.
3 · Fallback is instant. The skeleton is for LOADING a known photo only; a null tutor_image renders the fallback immediately, never a permanent shimmer (fixes the live gap).
4 · Photography policy. Real, authentic, diverse, Malaysian faces; no illustration for people; crop to face-and-shoulders in circles; specimens and mocks use labelled placeholders.
5 · One treatment per surface. Circles everywhere structural; the tinted hero is reserved for emotional moments (public-profile preview, milestones), never on triage cards.
