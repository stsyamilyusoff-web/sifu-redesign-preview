## The placement grammar (founder-ratified 11-Jul-2026)

One sentence carries the whole law: **the left edge says WHAT and WHETHER; the right edge says SO-WHAT.**

1. **Leading = identity + selection.** The left edge holds what a row IS - avatar, subject glyph, photo thumb - and the checkbox-selection mark, which rides with the thing it marks. A left tick-column reads as a runnable checklist. Convention base: iOS edit-mode, Gmail, WhatsApp; in-corpus exemplar Airbnb #110.
2. **Trailing = status + consequence.** The right edge holds what a row MEANS for you now - status pills, money values, chevrons, per-row actions (Retake / Add), toggles. This clause NAMES logic already locked piecewise: the list-rows 3-zone anatomy, the context-card trailing zone, and the nav-header trailing rules all cite it.
3. **Paired buttons: dismiss LEFT ghost, commit RIGHT filled.** Already locked at the dialog board; now a clause of the law rather than a board-local rule.

**The test:** identity → left · consequence → right.

**Origin, for the record:** the founder connected two open questions - the dialog CTA order and the confirm-screen checkbox side - and asked for a once-and-for-all rule, the same pattern that produced the radius law. A references-chat spot-verify of selection placement across the full corpus rides non-blocking; an addendum lands here if counter-evidence surfaces.
