MatchLine · the green fit signal

Lane C candidate set. The base is already locked by Wave 1 + DR-007 and is NOT re-opened here: a quiet green text line (no fill) on the request card, always a SPECIFIC reason, never a generic "matches you"; green is rationed to fit only; colour always rides on icon + word. This sheet decides the four remaining forks: reason order, separator grammar, the check glyph, and the known-family wording.
Grounded in origin/main of sifututor_tutor (ca46f83, 01-Jul-2026): reasons the data can power TODAY: subject = subjects_taught vs subject_id (EDC flag) · schedule = availability_slots (weekday/weekend × morning/afternoon/evening) vs class_day + class_time · known family = the live "Recommended" logic (SameParent · SameStudent · SameLevel · SameSubject against the tutor's student list). Gated reasons: "area" has NO tutor-side preferred-cities field yet (Hafiz Q11) · "level" is only derivable from teaching history, no levels-taught field (Hafiz). Filter, not reason: gender (tutor_preference vs tutor gender) and mode are hidden by filters per DR-007, never celebrated as a match; note the live feed does not filter gender yet (integrator flag). No match signal exists on the live card today.

How the references do it

Airbnb

No personalized fit signal. Closest: "Guest favorite" / "Popular", a quiet white pill of aggregate social proof on the photo. Lesson: quiet and wordy beats a shouty badge.

Urban Company

No fit, match, or recommendation signal on any list card in the sample. Rating + price only.

Fresha

No per-card signal. Personalizes by SECTION ("Book again", "Trending"), which validates our "For me" filter chip as the complement, not the substitute.

Verdict: no reference app has this component. MatchLine is Sifututor DNA; the design authority is our own Wave 1 lock (.preview/wave1-requests-feed.html) + DR-007.

Rules to ratify (not visual forks, say if any feels wrong)

1 · Subject is the anchor. The line renders only when the SUBJECT matches; other reasons append to it. A schedule-only or area-only coincidence is noise, no line.
2 · Cap at 3 reasons. More reads as bragging and risks wrapping. Priority when trimming: subject, then level, then area, then schedule.
3 · Gender and mode are never reasons. DR-007: what you can never take is hidden by a filter, not celebrated. Integrator flag: the live feed does not filter gender yet.
4 · Gated reasons ship dark. "Area" waits for the tutor-side preferred-cities field (Hafiz Q11); "level" rides the teaching-history rule (SameLevel) until a levels-taught field exists. The component accepts them as data, the feed just cannot send them yet.
5 · One MatchLine per card, one line tall. Truncate never; trim reasons instead (rule 2).
6 · Colour. Text + glyph #166534 on the white card, no fill, no border. Green stays rationed: on one card you can see at most the MatchLine, the seal (if a person shows), and "Be the first to apply".
