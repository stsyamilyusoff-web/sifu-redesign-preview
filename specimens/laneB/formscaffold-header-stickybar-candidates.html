Reference evidence + consolidation map

Fresha (Oct 2024, checkout): minimal bar (X close), large titles in content; confirm dock = summary left (price bold + grey meta) + dark 48pt button right, hairline top.
Urban Company (May 2026, checkout): back-arrow bar with inline title; docks = full-width filled 48pt CTA over a hairline, sometimes with a context line above.
Held from the lock: button atom (48/14, indigo primary, black neutral) in every dock; locked FormField anatomy inside FormScaffold; Phosphor arrow-left replaces the Entypo chevron (reconciled hub v0.17; the caret first drawn here is superseded); DR-002 paddings; the L2 banner drops into FormScaffold's banner slot.
