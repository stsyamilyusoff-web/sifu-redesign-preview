RequestCard · the density ladder (measured)

The founder's question: how does the ENHANCED card get SHORTER than the live one, so more requests fit per screen, while following our principles? Answer: skin polish cannot shrink the live card, its height is structural (three label-value rows + a padded chip row + double dividers). The principles we locked already prescribe the fix: DR-002 "density scales with item count, lean on many-item screens, ONE hero value" + DR-004 "rich complete rows in fixed scan zones" + the compact triage-density rule. Below: the same request, every field kept, four rungs, each height MEASURED in this render at true 402px.
Pivot reconciliation: "enhance the live app" holds at the APP level: same screen, same flow, same API, same fields, no rebuild. But on THIS card slot-preservation and density are in direct conflict. The ladder below is FIELD-preserving, not slot-preserving: all 10 live facts keep a visible home on the card, only their rendering compresses. Nothing moves to the detail screen; nothing is dropped.
