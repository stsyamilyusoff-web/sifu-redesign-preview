title: Components

The component model is **one themed core, three apps** (the Grab pattern): a single kit of locked components consumed by every Sifututor surface. The base is the **live app enhanced**, never a greenfield: locked components are the enhancement kit applied on top of real structure.

- **[DNA components](/sifu-redesign-preview/components/dna/)**: the locked atomic identity pieces.
- **[Forms and containers](/sifu-redesign-preview/components/forms-containers/)**: the Lane B locked specimens.
- **[Reuse registry](/sifu-redesign-preview/components/registry/)**: what to reuse from @sifu/ui and the no-raw-hex rule.
