## The placement grammar (founder-ratified 11-Jul-2026)

One sentence carries the whole law: **the left edge says WHAT and WHETHER; the right edge says SO-WHAT.**

1. **Leading = identity + selection.** The left edge holds what a row IS - avatar, subject glyph, photo thumb - and the checkbox-selection mark, which rides with the thing it marks. A left tick-column reads as a runnable checklist. Convention base: iOS edit-mode, Gmail, WhatsApp; in-corpus exemplar Airbnb #292-293 "Choose listings" (host guidebook).
2. **Trailing = status + consequence.** The right edge holds what a row MEANS for you now - status pills, money values, chevrons, per-row actions (Retake / Add), toggles. This clause NAMES logic already locked piecewise: the list-rows 3-zone anatomy, the context-card trailing zone, and the nav-header trailing rules all cite it. In-corpus support: Airbnb #110 filter steppers, Grab #162 trailing toggles.
3. **Paired buttons: dismiss LEFT ghost, commit RIGHT filled.** Already locked at the dialog board; now a clause of the law rather than a board-local rule. Overwhelming in-corpus: Fresha #56/#92, Urban Company Reset|Apply, Grab #116.

**The test:** identity → left · consequence → right.

**Origin, for the record:** the founder connected two open questions - the dialog CTA order and the confirm-screen checkbox side - and asked for a once-and-for-all rule, the same pattern that produced the radius law.

## Addenda (11-Jul-2026, spot-verify verdict: LAW HOLDS)

The references-chat sweep of selection placement across the corpus confirmed the law, with three footnotes:

- **Sub-rule, resolved: the checkbox ALWAYS leads.** On rows that already carry an avatar or thumbnail, the row content shifts right to make room (the iOS edit-mode way) - settled de facto by the founder's Confirm-classes pick. The trailing-checkbox variant (seen at Airbnb #292) is explicitly **disallowed**, so the two patterns never mix.
- **A documented exception that is NOT ours:** Grab #221 inverts button roles on destructive confirms (safe Cancel filled-right, destructive action ghost-left) - a legitimate alternative safety strategy, recorded here so nobody blends it with our locked ruling (commit-right + the earned red at the point of no return). One screen, one strategy: ours.
- **The law caught a live-code violation on arrival:** the shipping Filter screen renders Save LEFT / Reset RIGHT (Screens/Filter/index.tsx, two 48% CustomButtons - verified in code). Logged to the deferred live-code list: flip to Reset-ghost-left / Save-filled-right at next touch.
