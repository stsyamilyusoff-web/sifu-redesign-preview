title: Density grammar
description: DR-002, the locked density rules for lists and feeds.
---

Triage and feed rows stay **compact**, with rich, complete metadata in **fixed scan zones**; rows beat cards for triage. The locked reference is the [RequestCard density ladder](https://stsyamilyusoff-web.github.io/sifu-redesign-preview/specimens/laneC/requestcard-density-ladder.html).
