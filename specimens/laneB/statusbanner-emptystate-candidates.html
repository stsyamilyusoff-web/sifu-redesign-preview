Reference evidence + consolidation map

Airbnb (Jun 2025, Trips): banner = white outlined card, radius ~12, solid blue circular icon bubble 44pt, bold title + grey body, no background tint. Empty states: bold title + body + outlined CTA.
Fresha (Oct 2024): empty states inside hairline cards: title 22, grey body, outlined pill CTA; favourites variant adds a small illustration. Status markers are filled amber pills (our locked StatusPill atom's job, not banners).
Urban Company (May 2026): prompts are quiet grey rows with a trailing action button (WhatsApp alerts row); empty results = icon + text, minimal.
Held from the lock: radius 16 cards, locked button atom for CTAs (48/14, indigo primary, black neutral), amber = the one flag, green = fit/verified only, Phosphor icons, statusStyles amber/red/green tints kept as tokens. Re-grounded: info tint moves from the old #034BD9 blue to locked indigo. Retired: red retry button (retry is not destructive), the DripPromptBanner's PattensBlue, the retry chip's one-off #B45309 ambers (fold into the warning tokens).
Grounding, live app: shared StatusBanner (4 states), NotificationPermissionBanner, DripPromptBanner (dismiss x + CTA), InlineTip (kept as the quiet form-level cousin, icon 14 + bodySmall), DemandSummaryCard (chrome joins the info banner family; its content stays a Home DNA piece), EmptyState (~6 screens), ErrorState (~6 screens), TutorRequests illustrated empties, form warning boxes and retry chips, toast.ts (kept as the transient layer; visual pass later, not P1). Nothing dropped: dismiss x, action link, retry, illustration, inline tip and toast affordances all have homes above.
