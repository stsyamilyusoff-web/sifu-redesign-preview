## The placement grammar (founder-ratified 11-Jul-2026, revised same day at the Confirm-classes eyeball)

One sentence carries the whole law: **the left edge says WHAT; the right edge says SO-WHAT and WHETHER.**

1. **Leading = identity.** The left edge holds what a row IS - avatar, subject glyph, photo thumb. Uncontested through every revision.
2. **Trailing = status + consequence + selection.** The right edge holds what a row MEANS for you now - status pills, money values, chevrons, per-row actions (Retake / Add), toggles - **and the selection checkbox**: inclusion is a consequence-side fact ("checkboxes on the right, not left" - founder, at the render). In-corpus exemplar for trailing selection: Airbnb #292-293 "Choose listings". The iOS shift-right/leading-checkbox variant is **disallowed**, so the two patterns never mix. Further trailing support: Airbnb #110 filter steppers, Grab #162 toggles. This clause NAMES logic already locked piecewise: the list-rows 3-zone anatomy, the context-card trailing zone, and the nav-header trailing rules all cite it.
3. **Paired buttons: dismiss LEFT ghost, commit RIGHT filled.** Already locked at the dialog board; now a clause of the law rather than a board-local rule. Overwhelming in-corpus: Fresha #56/#92, Urban Company Reset|Apply, Grab #116.

**The test:** identity → left · consequence (including whether it is included) → right.

**Origin, for the record:** the founder connected two open questions - the dialog CTA order and the confirm-screen checkbox side - and asked for a once-and-for-all rule, the same pattern that produced the radius law.

## The checkbox arc, kept honestly (same treatment as the radius story)

The law was ratified with **checkbox-leads** (the iOS edit-mode reading: a left tick-column as a runnable checklist), and the corpus spot-verify initially pinned that sub-rule, treating the founder's Confirm-classes draft pick as settling it. **At the 1:1 eyeball the founder reversed it**: selection is a consequence, so the checkbox belongs on the right - and the exemplar map flipped with it (Airbnb #292-293 trailing checkboxes, previously marked the disallowed variant, became OURS; the shift-right variant became the disallowed one).

Process lesson, recorded verbatim into the law's history: **a draft pick is not a lock.** The "resolved de facto by the draft pick" inference was premature - rulings on placement happen at the eyeball, on the render.
